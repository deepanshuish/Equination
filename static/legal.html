Disclaimer & privacy

Not investment advice

Equination is an educational screening tool. It does not provide investment advice, does not place orders, and is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst. The stocks it lists are the output of fixed, published rules applied to market data; they are not recommendations tailored to you. Past performance, whether in a backtest or the live track record, does not guarantee future results. Trading equities involves risk of loss, including loss of principal. Decide for yourself, and consider consulting a registered adviser.

Your data

  • Your email and a salted password hash are stored to run your account.
  • Your Upstox API secret, Upstox access token and Marketaux token are stored encrypted at rest and used only to fetch data for your own scans. They are never shown back to you or to anyone else, and you can remove them at any time from Settings.
  • Market data fetched through your keys (prices, ratios, shareholding, news) is cached and shared with other users of this instance to reduce API usage. Nothing about your account, settings, picks or trades is shared.
  • Equination never places, modifies or cancels orders. The Upstox permissions it uses are read-only market data and your profile name for verification.

Third parties

Market data comes from Upstox and news from Marketaux under their own terms. Equination is not affiliated with either.

How it works

1

Connect Upstox

Create a free developer app on Upstox and log in once a day. Equination pulls daily candles, key ratios and shareholding patterns through your key — nothing is scraped.

2

Scan at the close

Every weekday at a time you choose, it looks for the pullback-reversal setup: 50 DMA over 200 DMA, a 3–12% dip, RSI(2) oversold, then a hammer, engulfing, piercing or morning-star candle on volume.

3

Gate on quality

Loss-makers, over-leveraged names, promoters cutting stake, pledged shares and clusters of negative news are removed before anything reaches your list.

4

Get a plan, not a tip

Each pick shows the entry, a stop under the pattern low, a target at the recent high or 2× risk, a hold-until date and a position size that risks ~1% of your capital.

Three views of the same scan

Quant

Numbers only. Technical score, ratio gates, position sizing. Nothing qualitative.

Sentiment

News only. Marketaux articles scored per company with a 3-day half-life, plus a whole-market sweep of the most positive and most negative names.

Cumulative

The blend: quant percentile, confidence-weighted sentiment and a fundamentals quality score, with weights you control.

The honest part

No strategy makes a fixed return every month. The setup Equination trades has, historically, a win rate around 55–65% with winners slightly larger than losers. That compounds to a realistic 1–3% a month on average — with losing months — only if you take the trades consistently and respect the stops.

Backtest and track record are built in. Run the rules on years of your own downloaded data before trusting them, and watch the live performance tab grade every pick the app has made. If the numbers aren't there, don't trade it.

You bring the data. Upstox's API and Marketaux's free tier are enough; your keys are stored encrypted and used only for your scans.

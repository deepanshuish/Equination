Strategy & schedule

How the picks are chosen

Mode 1 — 4-day swing: quality pullback + reversal candle

Finds fundamentally sound stocks in an uptrend that have just pulled back for a few days and printed a bullish reversal candle. Historically this is the short-horizon setup with the best documented odds; the Backtest tab measures those odds on your own data. Every pick comes with an entry, stop, target and a "hold until" date.

1. Quality gate (Upstox key ratios)

  • Return on equity ≥ 10%, positive earnings (P/E > 0 and < 80), positive net margin.
  • Debt/equity ≤ 1.5 (skipped for banks, NBFCs and insurers, where leverage is the business).
  • Missing data is flagged as "unverified"; tick the setting to exclude such stocks entirely.

2. Promoter gate (Upstox shareholding pattern)

  • Excluded: promoters cut their stake by ≥ 1.5 percentage points over the last two quarters, or pledged holdings > 15% where reported.
  • Flagged: promoter holding below 25%; promoters adding is shown as a positive.
  • Your own exclude list in Settings always wins.

3. Technical setup

  • Uptrend: 50 DMA above 200 DMA, price above 200 DMA and within 5% of or above the 50 DMA, positive 6-month return.
  • Pullback: 3–12% below the 20-day high and RSI(2) dipped under 25 within the last 3 sessions.
  • Trigger candle today: morning star, bullish engulfing, piercing line, hammer, bullish harami, or a strong close (green, top quarter of range, above yesterday's high, volume ≥ 1.2× average).
  • Sanity: ATR between 1% and 5% of price, liquid, not up more than 25% in the last month.
  • Regime: Nifty 50 above its 200-day average, otherwise quantities go to zero.

4. Ranking and trade plan

Score = 35% trend quality (6-month return ÷ volatility) + 20% pullback depth + 30% candle strength + 15% volume. Stop = just under the pattern's low (max 2.5 ATR). Target = the 10-day high, or 2× the risk if that is closer. Exit at the stop, the target, or the close of the last holding day, whichever comes first.

What to expect

Short-horizon setups are a numbers game: a good result is a 58–65% win rate with winners a bit larger than losers, which compounds to the 1–3% a month you are targeting only if you take the trades consistently and respect the stops. No fundamental or promoter check can see the next quarterly result or a surprise announcement — the stop is what protects you from those.

Mode 2 — Positional: trend-filtered, volatility-adjusted momentum rotation

Positional equity strategy aiming for a realistic 1-3% per month on average, with some losing months. It never trades intraday and no orders are placed by this app.

1. Market regime (Nifty 50)

  • RISK ON: Nifty above its 50 and 200-day averages: full allocation.
  • CAUTION: above 200-day, below 50-day: half allocation.
  • RISK OFF: below 200-day: no new buys. Momentum's worst losses happen in bear markets.

2. Eligibility filters

  • At least 260 daily bars, price above the minimum, 20-day average turnover above the minimum.
  • Stock above its own 50 and 200-day averages and within 25% of its 52-week high.
  • Annualised volatility below 60%; last-month return below 35% (avoids buying blow-off tops).

3. Ranking

score = (0.40 × R12-1 + 0.35 × R6 + 0.25 × R3) / volatility63d. R12-1 is the 12-month return skipping the latest month, the classic academic definition that sidesteps short-term mean reversion. Dividing by volatility prefers smooth trends.

4. Position sizing and exits

  • Stop-loss at 2.5 × ATR(14) below the close. Quantity = min(risk budget ÷ distance to stop, equal-weight slot).
  • Exit when a close is below the stop or the 50-day average, or when the stock drops out of the top 2N at the monthly review. Replace with the highest-ranked name you do not hold.
  • Rebalance monthly; check daily only for stop hits. Expect 2-6 trades a month with 10 positions.

Why this one

Momentum is the most persistent return anomaly documented across markets, including India, and it stacks well with trend filters. Its weakness, sharp drawdowns during regime changes, is exactly what the Nifty filter and volatility scaling address. The same fundamentals and promoter gates are applied to the shortlist before the final ranking. Run the backtest on your own downloaded data before trusting it, and remember that past performance does not guarantee future results.
